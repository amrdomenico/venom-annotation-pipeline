# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

This project is a scientific tool built for the venomics and proteomics community. We are committed to maintaining a welcoming and respectful environment for everyone who wants to use, discuss, or contribute to it. This includes people of all levels of programming experience, career stages, institutional affiliations, nationalities, and backgrounds, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, sexual identity and orientation, or religion.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive in issues, pull requests, and discussions
- Assume good faith. Most questions and suggestions come from genuine scientific curiosity or practical need
- Give credit where it is due; acknowledge when someone's suggestion improved your work
- Keep discussions focused on the science and the code

## Unacceptable behavior

- Dismissive or condescending remarks, especially toward researchers who are not experienced developers
- Personal attacks, harassment, or discriminatory language of any kind
- The use of sexualized language or imagery and unwelcome sexual attention or advances
- Claiming credit for others' contributions
- Deliberately posting incorrect classification rules or data to mislead other researchers
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces: GitHub issues, pull requests, commit messages, and any other communication directly related to this repository.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please open a private contact with the repository maintainer via GitHub or reach out through the contact information in the [CITATION.cff](https://github.com/amrdomenico/venom-annotation-pipeline/blob/main/CITATION.cff) file.

Reports will be handled with discretion and confidentiality.

## Enforcement

The maintainer reserves the right to remove comments, close issues, or block contributors who repeatedly or severely violate these guidelines.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
